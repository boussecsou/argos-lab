# Task 01 — Dependency Pinning

Produce a 500–700 word publishable, evidence-backed article for senior software engineers explaining why reproducible experiments require pinned dependency versions.

The article must distinguish reproducibility from general reliability, explain the risk of mutable branch references, include a practical five-item checklist, and acknowledge at least one cost of strict pinning. Prefer primary technical sources. Use a clear, pragmatic editorial voice.
